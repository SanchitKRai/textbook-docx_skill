## Cell components

A cell contains multiple structures:

- Plasma membrane
  - Selectively permeable boundary
  - Maintains cellular homeostasis
    - Regulates transport
- Cytoplasm
- Nucleus

1. Prepare the sample.
   1. Fix the cells.
   2. Apply stain.
2. Observe using a microscope.
3. Record the results.

| Structure | Function | Status |
|:---|:---:|---:|
| Nucleus | DNA storage | Present |
| Ribosome | Protein synthesis | Present |
| Cell wall | Protection | Variable |

> **Key concept:** The plasma membrane is selectively permeable.
> It regulates the movement of substances into and out of the cell.
> This is essential for maintaining internal balance.
